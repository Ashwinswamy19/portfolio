Research · Nov 2025 to May 2026

Payload Responsive EV Chassis

Independent author, with Accelo Mobility, an EV startup backed by SINE IIT Bombay

Published

The problem

I found a gap in commercial EV platforms. Most are retrofitted from petrol and diesel vehicles, and their rigid frames can't cope with loads that change at every delivery stop. That causes instability, faster wear and a real risk of battery fires.

What I did

I designed an optimised semi spaceframe chassis in CAD and ran more than 100 FEA iterations across the load conditions a delivery vehicle actually sees. I traded reinforcement against weld access and material cost so the design stays manufacturable and affordable in emerging markets.

The outcome

My design increased the safety factor by 4.6x over a ladder frame baseline. The research was published in the National High School Journal of Science, and I presented it at the Sigma Xi Student Research Conference. The project was also covered by The Economic Times (ET Education).

Independent Research · 2025 to 2026

Decentralised Finance and Payment Infrastructure

Independent economics research

John Locke Commendation

The question

I wanted to know whether a cashless economy is risky in itself, or whether the risk depends on who controls the payment infrastructure.

My argument

I compared payment systems in India, China, Nigeria and Sweden, and argued that control is what matters. I then extended the analysis to stablecoins, looking at how money moving outside banks could weaken monetary policy and dollarise deposits in emerging markets.

The outcome

My essay received a Commendation in Economics at the 2026 John Locke Essay Competition.

Internship · July 2026 · London, UK

Tech Mahindra

Platform economics client project for Bentley Motors

Client Report

The brief

I analysed Bentley's commercial strategy for sharing a car platform with Porsche, and how the cancellation of a shared chassis platform affected its operating profit.

My analysis

I looked at how shared platforms fix suspension hard points and standardise aesthetics, which constrains the thing Bentley's customers value most. I benchmarked Bentley's platform strategy against peer OEMs.

What I delivered

I wrote a client report proposing a new pricing model based on production volume, to help decide when sharing car platforms is profitable.
